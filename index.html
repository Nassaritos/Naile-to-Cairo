How to play this, in one paragraph

English is the working language; Turkish is what makes the shortlist short. The English-only roles — Vodafone, Foundever, Capgemini reporting, the UN posts — are where the career is, but also where she competes with a very large Cairo talent pool. The Turkish-tagged roles have perhaps a dozen qualified applicants in the whole country. So: apply to the Turkish ones for speed and a work permit, apply to the English ones for the trajectory, and don't treat them as separate searches.

Priority cards deserve a tailored application — a rewritten opening paragraph and a CV pointed at that job. Everything else can take the standard version. Fifteen careless applications lose to four careful ones.
